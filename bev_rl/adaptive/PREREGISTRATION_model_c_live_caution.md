# Pre-registration: Live Caution camera model (Model C) — retention calibration, picture-based caution sensor, gates

**Status:** DRAFT written 2026-11-15 for the user's review. It becomes binding only when the user approves it. Its SHA-256 is recorded in `PROVENANCE.json` of every retention output folder (a missing or changed file stops all workers).
**Scope:** only what is decided *before* any retention data exist. Nothing below was tuned on retention results.

## 1. Aim and design
Model C (primary): the privileged teacher receives the true calibrated caution `c`; the camera student receives `ĉ`, estimated from the weather-processed front-left picture only (no weather label, no simulator state).
Model B (fallback, table lookup of `c`) is used only if Gate 1 or Gate 2 fails; that outcome is reported first and the user decides.
Questions: (1) is the picture-based `ĉ` accurate on unseen episodes; (2) does it generalise to unseen weather levels and probes; (3) does driving with `ĉ` help (Gate 3, exploratory).

Seed-audit limitations (to be reproduced in the report): the seeds were chosen by availability, not by a stratified audit of road layout or traffic; episodes are generated by one scenario generator; the 30+10 episodes are a small sample of it; confidence intervals are episode-clustered and therefore wide for rare conditions.
Valid MetaDrive seeds are 10–10009, so every seed above is valid.

## 3. Conditions
Eleven: clear; fog 0.3, **0.45\***, 0.6, 0.8, 1.0; rain 0.2, **0.3\***, 0.45, 0.6, 1.0 (\* = probes). Probes are measured and reported; their targets never enter the table, the standardisation, the ridge fit, the dead-zone or any statistic used for fitting. They are scored only against their own raw cell.

## 4. Retention protocol (unchanged definition)
* Teacher A (`safe_g4.0`: SafeTeacher + curve guard 4.0) drives clear episodes, density 0.05, horizon 600, **action noise 0.0** (decision of 2026-11-15). The weather augmentation of the live pipeline is unchanged: fresh noise per step from `np.random.seed(20261114 + global_seed·100003 + t)`.
* Step convention: `t` = number of `env.step` calls so far. **t = 0 is never captured** (the camera rig is mounted after MetaDrive's reset render; precautionary exclusion; the live pipeline has the same artefact). Smoothing starts at t = 1. The initial step is excluded from evaluation.
* Every 5th step with t ≥ 5 the five raw frames are captured once, frozen (read-only arrays, SHA-256 before/after), and processed through all 11 conditions with the **existing YOLO + weather module**: five `apply_weather` calls in `CAMERA_RIGS` order, then `apply_synchronized_stereo_weather` for the front pair; stereo SGBM depth; YOLOv8n (conf 0.4) on four views; unmodified `objects_from_detections`; frozen `CameraGridBuilder` v2 (`corrected_objects`, `build`).
* Truth is used for measurement only: simulator vehicles in the window x∈[−10,10], y∈[−2.5,17.5].
* Matching: greedy one-to-one nearest-first, radius 3.0 m (`pilot_camera_builder.match`).
* Retention frames: ≥1 truth vehicle in the window, at most 40 per episode. Vehicle-free frames: 0 truth vehicles, at most 5 per episode, **analysis only** (valid-depth share, YOLO confidence, false-positive objects); they never replace vehicle-based retention or recall.
* **Calibration target (approved definition):** retention(cond) = Σ matched(cond) / Σ matched(clear) on identical frames; `c = clip(1 − retention, 0, 1)`. The retired box-count `retention_table.json` is not used.
* Separate metrics, never calibration targets: paired-identity retention = |M_clear ∩ M_cond| / Σ matched(clear); recall = Σ matched / Σ truth (the clear row is the detection ceiling); duplicates, false positives and invalid-depth drops, counted separately.
* The table is raw and unsmoothed, from training episodes only, nine non-probe conditions (clear has c = 0 by construction).

## 5. Statistical rules
* 2,000 episode-clustered bootstrap resamples (seed 20261115), 95% percentile intervals, one shared index matrix. Frames are never treated as independent episodes.
* A resample with zero clear-matched vehicles has no ratio: it is excluded and counted. **Valid resamples < 1,900 → the condition is flagged `bootstrap_unreliable`.**
* `assessable` is true only if the c-CI half-width ≤ 0.15 and the bootstrap is reliable; otherwise the condition is "not assessable" and is not scored.
* Coverage: ≥ 200 clear matched vehicles and ≥ 12 contributing episodes, reported for the first 20 training episodes (stop rule) and for all episodes, as booleans.

## 6. Sensor data (scope S-B) and its limits
* **Scope name: S-B-stride2** (decision of 2026-11-15, after the timing check; see §12). Held-out episodes (9040–9049): **all 11 conditions are kept**, but the 10 weather conditions are sampled **only at even steps t = 2, 4, 6, …** (`WEATHER_STREAM_STRIDE = 2`). Each sampled image is the final weather-processed front-left image produced by the full live weather block with the live noise seed for that t, so **at the sampled steps it is the image the live student would receive** (verified bit-for-bit at four diagnostic steps). Odd-step weather cells are recorded as *unsampled by design* (a boolean mask), never as missing or zero.
* Every episode (training and held-out): **clear-weather features at every step t ≥ 1**.
* Training episodes: weathered features only at the retention and vehicle-free frames (stride 5), taken from the same frames as the retention measurements.
* This is a **reduced scope relative to the original proposal S-A** (every-step weathered streams for all 40 episodes), which was infeasible in the time available, and relative to the first S-B (every-step held-out weather streams). Measured cost on seed 9000: 10.4 s per weathered step for the 10 weather conditions (≈ 1.7 h per held-out episode at every step); stride 2 halves this. Held-out weathered observations: ≈ 59,900 at every step → ≈ 29,900 at stride 2 (−50%). Training weather features are unchanged (retention and vehicle-free frames).
* **Temporal-sampling limitation (stated, not hidden):** Gate 1 evaluates the sensor's *sampled* temporal behaviour. The live sensor smooths at every step (α = 0.15 per step); the held-out weather data contain only every second step, so the smoothed output **between** sampled steps is not observed and **no claim is made that it equals the live smoothed output**. Weather noise is drawn independently at each step, so the live smoother averages twice as many noise samples as the sampled series; offline smoothing of the sampled series therefore has higher noise than live smoothing.
* Streams store **raw features only**. Raw per-frame predictions and smoothed predictions are computed later from fitted weights and written to separate, distinctly named files. Smoothing is applied per (episode, condition), is initialised at t = 1 and never carries state between episodes or conditions.
* Equivalence with the live student is verified, not assumed: at diagnostic steps (5, 50, 200, 400, those reached; ≥ 2 required) the front-left frame, front-right frame, the four YOLO inputs, the depth map, the 7-feature vector, the raw objects and the final grid are compared bit-for-bit with the genuine `student_bev()`, together with a negative control (the two-call "light" path must differ).

## 7. Sensor fitting rules (applied later, new files only; old sensor files are frozen)
* Features: the existing 7-feature function (`visibility_sensor.features`), imported read-only.
* Standardisation, ridge weights and the calibration target come from **training episodes only**, excluding probe targets. Held-out episodes and probe levels never enter any training statistic, standardisation parameter, ridge weight, dead-zone threshold or calibration value.
* **Sampling (accepted limitation):** weathered training features exist only on retention/vehicle-free frames, so the fit uses clear and weathered features from the **same** frames. The full clear stream is used only for the dead-zone and for temporal checks. Frames with ≥1 truth vehicle in the window (plus up to five vehicle-free frames) are not a random sample of driving; this selection effect is a documented limitation.
* The dead-zone is derived from training clear streams only.

## 8. Gates (mandatory before any DAgger)
* **Gate 1 (held-out 9040–9049, sampled temporal behaviour):** overall MAE ≤ 0.10 against the table `c`; every condition ≤ 0.15; ≥ 95% of clear frames with |ĉ − 0| ≤ 0.05 (thresholds unchanged). Smoothing is applied **per (episode, condition) to the sampled series only**: at weather conditions the exponential smoother is updated at the sampled steps with the two-step coefficient α₂ = 1 − (1 − 0.15)² = 0.2775 (fixed in advance), initialised at the first sampled step (t = 2); clear conditions are smoothed at every step with α = 0.15 from t = 1, exactly as live. Scoring uses the sampled steps. Gate 1 therefore assesses sampled temporal behaviour, **not** exact continuous per-step live behaviour; no equivalence of the smoothed output between sampled steps is claimed. The extra noise of the sampled series makes the weather part of Gate 1 conservative for the smoothed estimate, but a marginal failure is ambiguous and will be reported as such.
* **Gate 2 (episode-grouped cross-fitting, per-frame, unsmoothed):** five groups of six episodes; each fold withholds one weather level and its group from the fit; interior-level held-out MAE ≤ 0.15; median ĉ monotone in severity with Spearman ≥ 0.9; extremes (fog 1.0, rain 1.0) reported, not scored; conditions with c-CI half-width > 0.15 are "not assessable"; probes scored against their own raw cells; fog and rain separate; hashes of all inputs recorded before the test. **Gate 2 does not independently validate temporal smoothing** (no training weather streams exist under S-B).
* **Gate 3 (conditional, exploratory):** only if Gates 1 and 2 pass; seeds 9100–9104 × fog 0.6 and rain 0.45; arms ĉ / c = 0 / oracle c. Criteria (as agreed, filled in 2026-11-15):
  * D = absolute episode mean-speed difference from the revalidated Teacher B, per episode and arm.
  * Δ_use = D(c=0) − D(ĉ).
  * **Pass** if D(ĉ) − D(oracle) ≤ 0.5 m/s **and** Δ_use / [D(c=0) − D(oracle)] ≥ 0.75.
  * The test is **uninformative** if the denominator D(c=0) − D(oracle) is below 1.0 m/s (no verdict is drawn).
  * Estimated-caution failures (crash/off-road) must not exceed the c = 0 failures.
  * Uncertainty: 95% bootstrap over the 10 paired (seed × weather) episodes.
  * Gate 3 runs only after Gates 1 and 2 have passed.
* If Gate 1 or Gate 2 fails: stop and report; Model B is the fallback; the user decides.

## 9. Order after the retention pass
Retention (this file's protocol) → table + sensor fit + Gates 1–2 (hashes before test) → user review → Teacher B re-validation at the new `c` → relaunch pack and OV-student evaluation (unchanged scripts) → Live Caution DAgger rounds 0–3 (four rounds minimum; a fifth only with margin) → validation → final evaluation → Gate 3 (if eligible) → aggregation. The original 80-episode evaluation stays excluded.

## 10. Failure, recovery, exclusions
* A failed weather condition or incomplete frame aborts that episode; the exact stage, step, condition, exception and traceback are recorded in `failures/`. No condition is silently skipped.
* Claims of possibly active workers are never released automatically; only a claim its own worker marked `failed` can be re-claimed with `--retry-failed`. Completed episodes are never overwritten. A completion flag is created only if `validate` passes all checks.
* Exclusions: reserve seeds unless the stop rule fires and the user approves; episodes with an equivalence failure are not used until the cause is understood.
* Deviations from this document are logged in §12 with date and reason before any dependent analysis.

## 11. Provisional resource estimates (not commitments)
Measured by the one-worker timing check (seed 9000, 600 steps): training episode ≈ 13 min; held-out episode ≈ 2.1 min simulation + ≈ 1 min capture/clear features + ≈ 52 min weather stream at stride 2 (≈ 0.9 h); whole pass with one worker ≈ 16 h (training 6.4 h + held-out 9.1 h + ≈ 0.5 h overhead). One worker holds ≈ 4.7 GB (peak 6.1 GB during reset); three workers do not fit in 14 GB, two are unproven. Workers beyond one require the user's approval after review of measured memory. These remain estimates, not commitments.
Fallback Model B (table-lookup caution) needs only the 30 training episodes (≈ 6.4 h on one worker) and does not use the held-out streams; its DAgger is never run concurrently with retention collection.
