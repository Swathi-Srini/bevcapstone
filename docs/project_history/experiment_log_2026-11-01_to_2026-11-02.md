## 2026-11-02 Route-channel vs scalar lateral offset (M1)
Probe: BC-driven, seeds 1000–1002, 120 steps, every 10 steps (33 samples). Route-centreline x at ego y=0 vs scalar `lateral_deviation`: corr = -0.995, mean(sum) = +0.007 m, i.e. route_x = -lateral (opposite sign convention, same magnitude). Result: no bug. The +0.51 m "offset" is real BC drift: BC creeps to ~0.8–0.96 m from lane centre by step ~80–100 (seed 1001 reached 1.58 m at step 80). Consequence: BC baseline has a systematic lateral bias; a failure case to report. Script (scratchpad): route_probe.py.

## 2026-11-02 Perception cost and native half-resolution rendering (M2)
Benchmark (bev_rl/benchmark_perception.py, 30 steps, seed 1000, no traffic): full-res 1200x900 + YOLO: 1.7 s (clear) / 2.1 s (fog 0.6) / 2.5 s (rain 0.6) per step, RSS ~4 GB; dominated by YOLO (0.8–1.1 s), weather (0.7–1.2 s), render (0.25 s). PPO at that cost infeasible (100k steps ~ 50+ h).
Changes: cameras rendered natively at 600x450 (FOV/focal geometry unchanged; this is the half-res the BEV pipeline already used), fog transmission cached, morphology cropped to cell-footprint ROI (equivalence test, max diff 3 grey levels), YOLO disabled in zero-traffic training (obstacle channel empty by construction there). Result: 0.20 s (clear) / 0.39 s (fog 0.6) / 0.52 s (rain 0.6) per step, RSS ~3.5 GB.
Validity check (validate_bev --cam-scale 0.5, seed 1000, 10 frames): clear road acc 0.946 (full-res 0.945), road IoU 0.512 (0.511), boundary P/R 0.76/0.54 (0.84/0.51), line P/R 0.88/0.54 (0.91/0.60); fog 1.0 known-observable 0.862 (0.853); rain 1.0 boundary precision 0.36 (0.38); 0 nondeterministic rebuilds. => comparable; classifier not retrained.
Note: frames from MetaDrive are BGR (frame_from_sensor); classifier and weather use that ordering consistently; the fog airlight is thus (B,G,R)=(200,205,210) i.e. slightly warm instead of cool haze; recorded as a limitation.
